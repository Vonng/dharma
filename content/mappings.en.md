## How to Use This Table

1. Choose a concrete engineering problem, not a favored tradition.
2. Read the primary entry and identify its core model.
3. Use the cross-reading links to look for conflicts, counterexamples, and boundaries.
4. Return to the actual system and test the analogy against observable behavior; a metaphor is not proof.

The seven volumes do not always reconcile. Preserving those tensions is the point of the map.
